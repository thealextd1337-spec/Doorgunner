# Doorgunner

Top-down helicopter rescue roguelike. You are the door gunner of a military helicopter
over a zombie-infested countryside: rescue survivors, haul them to the forward base,
build the base up, and hold it when the horde assaults every third night.

## Play
- Open the project in Godot 4.7 (`godot --path .`) and press F5, or run `godot --path . --export`-free: just `godot --path .`.
- **WASD / arrows** fly, **mouse** aims, **LMB** fires the door gun (out of the side doors only: swing the heli to bring it to bear; zombies hide in forests and chew into houses), **SPACE** lands / takes off, **Q / E** swing the heli (the door guns only fire sideways), **Q** switches guns, **F** fires the loudspeaker (when fitted), **R** restarts after a run ends, **ESC** opens the sound settings (volumes are saved).
- Land next to a point of interest to interact: settlements (survivors board one by one, hold the LZ; settlements and hideouts inside the fence or within 12 m of it walk to the base by themselves and move in),
  supply crates (fuel, ammo, scrap), weapons caches (a weapon or one item card), relay towers (reveal nearby POIs),
  crash sites (scrap and a permanent boost). Zombie dens spawn an abomination boss when approached.
- The run starts without a base: the heli carries a founding crew of 4, 60 scrap and basic supplies. Pick a site with room for the 5x5 helipad (fields and town edges are fine, ideally near water), land and press **B** to found the FOB there: helipad, field workshop, the crew moves in (2 on Build), and the first assault comes two nights later. Until then there is nowhere to refuel or drop survivors.
- Landing on the base pad refuels, rearms, repairs, delivers passengers and opens the base menu (turrets, depot, armory, workshop, buildings, perimeter).
- The base has needs: everyone eats 1 food and drinks 1 water a day; with no beds left (2 in the hangar, 4 per Barracks) people camp outside, eat double, work at half rate and get hurt in assaults. Buildings (Barracks, Field Clinic, Generator, Well, Watchtower) and perimeter stages are placed as blueprints in the BUILD tab inside the fence and built by Build workers. The injured need a clinic bed and 1 medicine (pharmacies, crates, crash sites) or may die; water comes from wells, the Water job (river or lake nearby), water towers in towns, crates and lake huts. A fuelled Generator lights the base at night, keeps turrets at full rate, speeds healing and thins out the night hordes. Militia only shoot with rifles (weapons caches, crash sites, the Armory, zombie drops). The first run starts a short tutorial (also on the title screen; ESC menu skips it).
- Base life and menu: every rescued survivor (up to 60 drawn) steps off the pad, changes into uniform in the barracks (the hangar until one is built) and then goes about their job: militia patrol the sandbag line with rifles and stand on the defence posts during assaults, fuel/ammo/repair/workshop/build crews shuttle between hangar, depot and blueprints with a tool and pistol, farmers walk the fields, water carriers go to the river, medics stay by the clinic, the injured lie in it; at night everyone but the militia sleeps inside (campers by the fence). The base menu shows a status column on the left (population, stocks with per-day change, power, next assault) and BUILD / WORKERS / HANGAR tabs on the right; disabled buttons say why on hover (`scripts/base_pawn.gd`, `scripts/base_ui.gd`).
- People and morale (`scripts/people.gd`, `scripts/morale.gd`): every survivor is a named person (names from `data/names.txt`, fixed by the run seed) with a skill (Mechanic, Farmer, Medic, Soldier, Builder, Driver, rare Engineer) and maybe a trait (Tough, Glutton, Lazy, Brave, Insomniac). The WORKERS tab still sets how many work each job; the best-suited people take it (matching skill works x1.5, x1.75 at Lv 2 after 5 days, x2 at Lv 3 after 15; Soldiers on militia posts hit harder) and lists everyone (sort by job, name, skill or level; filter by job, skill, idle, injured or pinned; each row shows skill, trait, job, work rate); a click shows the person. A person can be pinned to a job in their row: they keep it (the job's count rises to cover them, and cannot go below its pinned people), the automatic assignment only fills the rest, an injured pinned person is off duty until healed, and Unassign all clears the pins. Base morale (0-100, start 60) is settled each day from hunger, thirst, campers, deaths, power, beds, assaults and big rescues; under 40 work slows, under 25 people may leave at night, over 75 work speeds up. The status column shows morale and its three main reasons; a full cabin shows who is aboard on the HUD.
- Salvage: crates, crash sites, scrap yards (industrial towns, some suburbs) and kills yield metal, parts and electronics; with the Workshop upgrade, Workshop workers break 12 units a day each down into scrap (electronics 3, parts 2, metal 1).
- Building: pick a tool in the BUILD tab and the menu gives way to a build bar along the bottom (**1-8** switch tools, **LMB** place, **RMB** remove / cancel, **R** rotates a lone sandbag, **ESC** back to the menu). A ghost on the 16 px grid shows what goes where, green if it fits, red if not, with its cost. Everything goes on open ground inside the fence (fields, yards and roads too) and is built by survivors on Build duty.
- Sandbags (5 scrap) join their neighbours into straight runs, corners and T pieces; zombies have to chew through them, Militia man them during assaults, Medics patch the hull for free. Barbed wire (3 scrap) can be walked through, but zombies in it crawl and bleed until it wears out. **Demolish** marks a house, hedge or garden fence inside the fence: Build workers tear it down for scrap (a house also gives metal and parts).
- Infected (`scripts/spawn_table.gd` holds every enemy type and its spawn weights by danger, day, region and town; `scripts/infected.gd` the abilities): **screamers** (day 3+) stop, wind up for 3 s and scream, which calls every zombie within ~600 px (shoot them first); **spitters** (day 4+) keep 80-120 px away and spit acid at sandbags, wire and a landed heli; **crawlers** (day 2+, forests and lakes) crawl through barbed wire unharmed and are a smaller target (use flak or splash); **bloaters** (day 5+, towns) burst into gas and hurt people in the base if they die at the fence; from day 10 **armored brutes** stop 70 % of shots into their front (flank them with Q/E) and horde spawns are sometimes runner packs. Hits knock zombies back, flash white and splatter; corpses stay for a minute (max 120); headshots do double damage (Optics raises the chance); heavy guns shake the camera (switch off in the ESC menu).
- Assaults (`scripts/assault.gd`, consts at the top): they come in 2-3 pushes with a pause to reload and patch the line. From the 2nd assault a theme (radio and HUD show its name): runner storm, brute ram (brutes walk straight at the base and smash sandbags and houses), screamer chain (more zombies keep coming while a screamer lives), spitter siege, mixed. The evening before, a Watchtower radios the main direction ("movement north-east") and the HUD draws an arrow at the fence; 70 % of the attackers come from there (with or without a tower). Every 9th night from founding is a **blood moon**: red sky, a boss for sure, a bigger wave, 2x XP, the night does not end until the last attacker is dead (at most 8 minutes of held clock), morale +10 once it is held. `Assault.night_taken(day)` says whether an assault falls on a day (bandit raids keep out of it).
- Bandits (`scripts/bandits.gd` camps and raids, `scripts/bandit.gd` riflemen, MG nests, RPG gunners, `scripts/technical.gd` pickups; unit counts in `scripts/spawn_table.gd`): from day 4 bandits hold prisons, checkpoints, motels and quarries, more of them the farther out and the later. Unlike zombies they shoot the heli in the air: keep moving and keep your distance (hits fall off with range, speed and darkness). Riflemen run for cover behind houses, hedges and woods, where half your rounds miss; an RPG gunner aims for 1.5 s (red line, radio warning), then fires a homing rocket: break hard or drop a flare (every flare level pulls rockets off). Technicals drive the roads with an MG. Bandits and zombies fight each other, and the gunfire draws more zombies. Camps send patrols that carry people off from settlements; intercepted bandit radio announces raids on settlements (dusk) and, from day 12, on the base (at night, never on a zombie assault night). Kill a camp's whole garrison to clear it for good: XP, +8 morale, freed prisoners to pick up and a stash (rifles, fuel, ammo, scrap) when you land there.
- Radio missions (`scripts/missions.gd`, list `scripts/missions_ui.gd`): from day 2, with a base, Command radios a mission every ~2.5 min (at most 3 open, **J** shows the list with direction, time left and reward; the HUD compass and minimap mark them; accepting means flying there, the timer running out drops it). **Rooftop rescue** (a family on a roof in a town: winch them or land in the yard), **Black box** (land at an airliner crash site; salvage and a key part), **Silence the screamer** (a screamer and guards near a town that calls a horde each night), **Escort** (refugees walk to the base while you stay close). Rewards: scrap, XP, morale, often a person with a rare skill (Engineer, Driver). **Clear the camp** and **Medicine run** are offered once the bandit and village systems exist (see the header of `missions.gd`). Missions save with the run.
- Pace is deliberate: landing and take-off spool for 1.5 s, gunfire and the rotor make NOISE that draws zombies (flying is safe, landing is not: everything within 380 px swarms a landed heli), settlements fall overnight if you leave them, the base eats food (farm workers, crates) and hull repairs cost scrap.
- Fuel and ammo run out. Dying ends the run; survivors rescued across runs unlock permanent seats, fuel and ammo capacity (saved per profile in `user://profiles/<id>/meta.json`; volumes stay global in `user://settings.json`).
- Ironman save (`scripts/save.gd`): the run autosaves (landing on the base pad, each new day, every 120 s, closing the window or hiding the browser tab; also in the middle of an assault, never during the tutorial) into `user://profiles/<id>/run.sav`. The title offers **CONTINUE**; **NEW RUN** over a saved run abandons it (counts as lost). Death deletes the save. New systems hook in with `Save.register(key, save_fn, load_fn)`; the world is replayed from the seed plus a chunk/founding event log (kept for POI ids; generation itself does not depend on load order) and a tile edit log (`chunks.edit`).
- Heli wear (`scripts/wear.gd`, constants at the top): rotor, engine, gearbox and door gun (condition 0-100 %) wear in flight and from hits (bandit hits three times as much). Under 50 % they bite (engine: slower, rotor: the gun spreads, gearbox: more fuel, gun: jams); an engine under 25 % may quit: 3 s of alarm, then an autorotation glide of 8-12 s with weak steering, **SPACE** puts her down (open ground costs some hull, forest, water or houses a lot; passengers may get hurt) and the crew repairs on site (45 s, faster with a mechanic or engineer aboard) before take-off with a 30 % engine. At the base the HANGAR tab shows the four bars and orders repairs: the base crew works while the heli sits on the pad and spends salvage parts (10 % per part).
- Progression (`docs/PROGRESSION.md`, `scripts/progression.gd`): the heli has hardpoint / system / module slots (start 1/2/2, Workshop or meta unlocks raise them to 2/3/3).
  Each level-up offers three item cards (new item or next level, max 5): **1-4** or click picks, **R** rerolls, **S** skips for 15 scrap, **B** banishes (unlocked at 400 rescued).
  Heli level caps at 30, further XP turns into scrap. The base HANGAR tab scraps items; the title screen HANGAR (**H**) lists every item and its unlock condition.

## Dev
- Version: `config/version` in `project.godot`, history in `CHANGELOG.md`. `bash tools/deploy_web.sh` exports the Web build to GitHub Pages, stamps it with commit and date and tags the commit `v<version>` (bump the version for a new release).
- `python tools/gen_sprites.py` regenerates the art ("Field Map" style) in `assets/sprites/b/`. Previews: `docs/region_*.png`.
- `python tools/gen_sfx.py` regenerates all sound effects in `assets/sfx/`. Sound starts muted for now: **M** or the ESC menu turns it on.
- Profiles and leaderboard on the title (`scripts/profiles.gd`, `profile_ui.gd`, `leaderboard_ui.gd`): the first start opens **ENLIST** (callsign checked live by the name filter, portrait from a 4x3 grid), **PROFILES** (or the portrait top left) switches, creates, renames and deletes profiles, **LEADERBOARD** (**L**) shows POINTS / DAYS / RESCUED / KILLS / BEST RUN with your own row highlighted and a click-through breakdown. Everything local works offline; a profile created or renamed offline is registered / renamed when `Online.online_changed(true)` fires, and the title shows `Online.title_notice()`.
- Player-name filter: `scripts/name_filter.gd` (`NameFilter.check(name)` returns "" or the reason). Word lists in `data/` (banned words partly from LDNOOBW, CC BY 4.0, see `data/ATTRIBUTION.md`); `python tools/gen_wordlist_sql.py` writes the matching server inserts to `server/supabase/zz_wordlists.sql`.
- Headless checks (bash): `SMOKE=1 godot --headless --path . --quit-after 30000` prints `SMOKE OK`. Other values: `poi`, `base`, `ui`, `prog` (heli systems), `tut` (tutorial), `nav` (pathfinding), `found` (founding the base; the other tests start with a base founded at the start site), `town` (world gen: a city, towns joined by road, road bends), `filter` (player-name filter), `save` (save, reload, load: same world and state; saves during an assault, old format discarded, death deletes), `assault` (schedule, themes, pushes, direction, Watchtower report, blood moon, save and load in the middle of an assault), `people` (named survivors, skills, morale), `world` (same chunks in any load order, site spacing, roads, frame time while flying; `WORLD_MAP=<png>` writes an overview), `infected` (new infected: abilities, spawn table, hit feedback, save/load of enemy state), `bandits` (camps and garrisons, technical on the road, hits on the heli, RPG and flares, bandits vs zombies, raids, save/load), `wear` (heli wear, engine failure, autorotation, emergency landing, repairs), `missions` (radio missions: offers, targets, rewards, expiry, save/load), `profiles` (create / switch / rename / delete profiles, name feedback, leaderboard screen), `online` (online client against the stub `tools/fake_supabase.py`, which the test starts itself; needs `python` on the PATH). Any `SMOKE=<name>` runs `tests/<name>_test.gd`; tests (`SMOKE`) use their own profile list in `user://profiles/_smoke/`, never the player's profiles.
- Online (`scripts/online.gd`, autoload `Online`, backend in `server/README.md`): anonymous Supabase sign-in (tokens in `user://profiles/<id>/auth.json`, refreshed, one retry on 401), `register` / `rename` / `set_avatar` / `delete_me`, `fetch_board` / `fetch_player`. A run is sent when it starts, at each new day and when it ends or is abandoned; if the first call at run start fails the run is **unranked** (saved with the run, never sent), a ranked run that loses the connection queues updates in `outbox.json`. Headless tests (`SMOKE`) never go online; `ONLINE_URL` overrides the server.
- World gen (`scripts/chunk_manager.gd`): seven regions (farmland, forest, lakeland, suburbs, wasteland, highland, river valley) with clearings, ponds, orchards, burned patches and copses inside. A macro grid of 5x5 chunks (~2560 px) holds one site each: a town (seven templates per region, rare cities with a 4-5 block street grid) or a landmark (gas station, military checkpoint, campground, quarry, airfield, radio mast, dam, crashed airliner, prison, motel, lumber camp, church ruin, highway with wrecks), or nothing; a town is always within ~1200 px of the start. Each site links to its two nearest neighbours by road (A* over terrain, paved between cities, fords over brooks, bridges over rivers). Gas stations, checkpoints and airfields have a fuel depot (fills the tank once). Generation is order-independent (`SMOKE=world`); other systems find sites with `chunks.sites_near(pos, radius, kind)`. Previews: `docs/region_*.png`, `docs/landmarks.png`.
- Walkers (zombies, survivors, base pawns) path around houses, hedges, water, rock, sandbags and buildings with `main.gd` `nav_move` / `nav_path` (cached AStarGrid2D on the 16 px grid, straight line when free); walled-in zombies chew through after 2 s.
- `SMOKE=shot godot --path .` saves screenshots (flight, combat, level-up cards, base, hangar, settings, title hangar) into the project folder. `SHOT_AT=suburb:city` (region, optionally `:template` or `:landmark`) starts them next to such a site, `SHOT_AT=airfield` next to the nearest landmark of that kind.
