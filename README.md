# Doorgunner

Top-down helicopter rescue roguelike. You are the door gunner of a military helicopter
over a zombie-infested countryside: rescue survivors, haul them to the forward base,
build the base up, and hold it when the horde assaults every third night.

## Play
- Open the project in Godot 4.7 (`godot --path .`) and press F5, or run `godot --path . --export`-free: just `godot --path .`.
- **WASD / arrows** fly, **mouse** aims, **LMB** fires the door gun (out of the side doors only: swing the heli to bring it to bear; zombies hide in forests and chew into houses), **SPACE** lands / takes off, **Q / E** swing the heli (the door guns only fire sideways), **Q** switches guns, **F** fires the loudspeaker (when fitted), **R** restarts after a run ends, **ESC** opens the sound settings (volumes are saved).
- Land next to a point of interest to interact: settlements (survivors board one by one, hold the LZ; settlements and hideouts inside the fence or within 12 m of it walk to the base by themselves and move in),
  supply crates (fuel, ammo, scrap), weapons caches (a weapon or one item card), relay towers (reveal nearby POIs),
  crash sites (scrap and a permanent boost). Zombie dens spawn an abomination boss when approached.
- The run starts without a base: the heli carries a founding crew of 4, 60 scrap and basic supplies. Pick a site with room for the 5x5 helipad (fields and town edges are fine, ideally near water), land and press **B** to found the FOB there: helipad, field workshop, the crew moves in (2 on Build), and the first assault comes two nights later. Until then there is nowhere to refuel or drop survivors.
- Landing on the base pad refuels, rearms, repairs, delivers passengers and opens the base menu (turrets, depot, armory, workshop, buildings, perimeter).
- The base has needs: everyone eats 1 food and drinks 1 water a day; with no beds left (2 in the hangar, 4 per Barracks) people camp outside, eat double, work at half rate and get hurt in assaults. Buildings (Barracks, Field Clinic, Generator, Well, Watchtower) and perimeter stages are placed as blueprints in the BUILD tab inside the fence and built by Build workers. The injured need a clinic bed and 1 medicine (pharmacies, crates, crash sites) or may die; water comes from wells, the Water job (river or lake nearby), water towers in towns, crates and lake huts. A fuelled Generator lights the base at night, keeps turrets at full rate, speeds healing and thins out the night hordes. Militia only shoot with rifles (weapons caches, crash sites, the Armory, zombie drops). The first run starts a short tutorial (also on the title screen; ESC menu skips it).
- Base life and menu: every rescued survivor (up to 60 drawn) steps off the pad, changes into uniform in the barracks (the hangar until one is built) and then goes about their job: militia patrol the sandbag line with rifles and stand on the defence posts during assaults, fuel/ammo/repair/workshop/build crews shuttle between hangar, depot and blueprints with a tool and pistol, farmers walk the fields, water carriers go to the river, medics stay by the clinic, the injured lie in it; at night everyone but the militia sleeps inside (campers by the fence). The base menu shows a status column on the left (population, stocks with per-day change, power, next assault) and BUILD / WORKERS / HANGAR tabs on the right; disabled buttons say why on hover (`scripts/base_pawn.gd`, `scripts/base_ui.gd`).
- Salvage: crates, crash sites, scrap yards (industrial towns, some suburbs) and kills yield metal, parts and electronics; with the Workshop upgrade, Workshop workers break 12 units a day each down into scrap (electronics 3, parts 2, metal 1).
- Building: pick a tool in the BUILD tab and the menu gives way to a build bar along the bottom (**1-8** switch tools, **LMB** place, **RMB** remove / cancel, **R** rotates a lone sandbag, **ESC** back to the menu). A ghost on the 16 px grid shows what goes where, green if it fits, red if not, with its cost. Everything goes on open ground inside the fence (fields, yards and roads too) and is built by survivors on Build duty.
- Sandbags (5 scrap) join their neighbours into straight runs, corners and T pieces; zombies have to chew through them, Militia man them during assaults, Medics patch the hull for free. Barbed wire (3 scrap) can be walked through, but zombies in it crawl and bleed until it wears out. **Demolish** marks a house, hedge or garden fence inside the fence: Build workers tear it down for scrap (a house also gives metal and parts).
- Pace is deliberate: landing and take-off spool for 1.5 s, gunfire and the rotor make NOISE that draws zombies (flying is safe, landing is not: everything within 380 px swarms a landed heli), settlements fall overnight if you leave them, the base eats food (farm workers, crates) and hull repairs cost scrap.
- Fuel and ammo run out. Dying ends the run; survivors rescued across runs unlock permanent seats, fuel and ammo capacity (saved per profile in `user://profiles/<id>/meta.json`; volumes stay global in `user://settings.json`).
- Ironman save (`scripts/save.gd`): the run autosaves (landing on the base pad, each new day, every 120 s, closing the window or hiding the browser tab; never during an assault or the tutorial) into `user://profiles/<id>/run.sav`. The title offers **CONTINUE**; **NEW RUN** over a saved run abandons it (counts as lost). Death deletes the save. New systems hook in with `Save.register(key, save_fn, load_fn)`; the world is replayed from the seed plus a chunk/founding event log and a tile edit log (`chunks.edit`).
- Progression (`docs/PROGRESSION.md`, `scripts/progression.gd`): the heli has hardpoint / system / module slots (start 1/2/2, Workshop or meta unlocks raise them to 2/3/3).
  Each level-up offers three item cards (new item or next level, max 5): **1-4** or click picks, **R** rerolls, **S** skips for 15 scrap, **B** banishes (unlocked at 400 rescued).
  Heli level caps at 30, further XP turns into scrap. The base HANGAR tab scraps items; the title screen HANGAR (**H**) lists every item and its unlock condition.

## Dev
- Version: `config/version` in `project.godot`, history in `CHANGELOG.md`. `bash tools/deploy_web.sh` exports the Web build to GitHub Pages, stamps it with commit and date and tags the commit `v<version>` (bump the version for a new release).
- `python tools/gen_sprites.py` regenerates the art ("Field Map" style) in `assets/sprites/b/`. Previews: `docs/region_*.png`.
- `python tools/gen_sfx.py` regenerates all sound effects in `assets/sfx/`. Sound starts muted for now: **M** or the ESC menu turns it on.
- Player-name filter: `scripts/name_filter.gd` (`NameFilter.check(name)` returns "" or the reason). Word lists in `data/` (banned words partly from LDNOOBW, CC BY 4.0, see `data/ATTRIBUTION.md`); `python tools/gen_wordlist_sql.py` writes the matching server inserts to `server/supabase/zz_wordlists.sql`.
- Headless checks (bash): `SMOKE=1 godot --headless --path . --quit-after 30000` prints `SMOKE OK`. Other values: `poi`, `base`, `ui`, `prog` (heli systems), `tut` (tutorial), `nav` (pathfinding), `found` (founding the base; the other tests start with a base founded at the start site), `town` (world gen: a city, towns joined by road, road bends), `filter` (player-name filter), `save` (save, reload, load: same world and state; assault defers, old format discarded, death deletes). Any `SMOKE=<name>` runs `tests/<name>_test.gd`; tests use their own profile `_smoke`.
- World gen (`scripts/chunk_manager.gd`): towns come in seven templates per region plus rare cities (a 4-5 block street grid with a plaza and church, park, warehouses and car parks, more survivors; suburbs and some farmland, never next to the start). Every town links by dirt lanes to its two nearest known towns / the base (Z or L shapes, A* round lakes, fords up to 5 cells); road bends and junctions get their own tiles.
- Walkers (zombies, survivors, base pawns) path around houses, hedges, water, rock, sandbags and buildings with `main.gd` `nav_move` / `nav_path` (cached AStarGrid2D on the 16 px grid, straight line when free); walled-in zombies chew through after 2 s.
- `SMOKE=shot godot --path .` saves screenshots (flight, combat, level-up cards, base, hangar, settings, title hangar) into the project folder. `SHOT_AT=suburb:city` (region, optionally `:template`) starts them next to such a town.
