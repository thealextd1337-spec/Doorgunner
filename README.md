# Doorgunner

Top-down helicopter rescue roguelike. You are the door gunner of a military helicopter
over a zombie-infested countryside: rescue survivors, haul them to the forward base,
build the base up, and hold it when the horde assaults every third night.

In short (1.1.0): fly sorties from a base you found yourself, land to pick up survivors, loot and salvage, and put
everyone to work (farm, water, fuel, ammo, militia, workshop, forge). Hold the nightly assaults with sandbags, wire,
turrets and the door gun (twelve guns to unlock), fight bandit camps and four kinds of bosses, and meet two named
rivals who learn how you play: a Warlord who ends up in a captured gunship and an Alpha who leads the horde. Radio
events ask for decisions, outposts, allied villages, convoys and repaired wrecks spread you over a map of farmland,
forest, river, highland, coast and winter regions, and from day 15 a Radio Station and an Airstrip open the
evacuation. One ironman run per profile, a daily seed and a weekly season on the leaderboard, mouse and keyboard or
touch.

## Play
- Open the project in Godot 4.7 (`godot --path .`) and press F5, or run `godot --path . --export`-free: just `godot --path .`.
- **WASD / arrows** fly, **mouse** aims, **LMB** fires the door gun (out of the side doors only: swing the heli to bring it to bear; zombies hide in forests and chew into houses), **SPACE** lands / takes off, **Q / E** swing the heli (the door guns only fire sideways), **Q** switches guns, **F** fires the loudspeaker (when fitted), **R** restarts after a run ends, **ESC** opens the sound settings (volumes are saved).
- **Touch** (switches on with the first touch and off again with mouse or keyboard input; "Touch controls" in the ESC menu shows and forces it; every start is in mouse mode): left stick flies, right stick aims and fires while held out, buttons left of the aim stick for land / take off, gun, yaw, loudspeaker, hook and jobs, context buttons (found base, outpost, protect) when they apply, MENU (under the minimap) for the ESC menu. In build mode a tap moves the preview and PLACE / REMOVE / ROTATE / DONE act on it. Landscape only. All input goes through `scripts/controls.gd` (autoload `Controls`: `move()`, `aim()`, `fire()`, `yaw()`, `point()`) and the Input actions of `Game._setup_input()`; nothing reads raw keys (`SMOKE=controls` checks). The web export uses `web/shell.html` (Godot's default shell plus no browser zoom / scroll and a fullscreen button).
- **Keys, help, tooltips** (Q6b): ESC menu > "Key bindings..." rebinds every action (`scripts/keymap.gd`, `scripts/keys_ui.gd`): click a key to replace it (keyboard or mouse button), **+** adds a second one, right-click removes one, shared keys that are not shared by default show in red, "Reset to defaults" undoes it; saved in `settings.json` ("keys"), WASD-style actions stay physical keys, hotkeys stay by keycode. **F1** (or ESC menu > "Help & codex") opens `scripts/codex_ui.gd`: the current bindings (plus the touch controls in touch mode) and the Enemies / Buildings / Items / Systems pages that fill from `Codex.register(category, id, title, text)` and `Codex.unlock(id)` (`scripts/codex.gd`, unlocked ids per profile in `meta.json`). Hover texts of the menus, jobs and buildings are one table in `scripts/tooltips.gd` (items and modifiers come from their own tables). The HUD hint line, the outpost / protect hints and the base menu toggle show the current keys (`KeyMap.tag(action)`); other key hints (e.g. "Mute all (M)", the tutorial, the level-up cards) are fixed text and do not follow a rebinding.
- **Onboarding and codex** (Q11, `scripts/onboarding.gd`, constants at the top): radio-guided chapters (assault, base & workers, missions, outposts & villages, bandits, convoys, wrecks, evacuation; 3-6 steps, each waits on a game condition) start on first contact, can be skipped from the card or turned off with "Tutorial hints & chapters" in the ESC menu; one-time context hints (injured passenger, gun jam, RPG, spitter, first night, low fuel ...) per profile in `meta.json` "onboarding"; key names follow the bindings, touch mode has its own texts. It is not `Game.world.tutorial`, so autosave keeps working. The F1 codex is filled from the single table `scripts/codex_content.gd` (enemies, buildings, items, systems) and unlocks on first encounter; other packages add rows with `Codex.register`, an enemy id `z_<kind>` / `b_<kind>` unlocks itself. `SMOKE=onboarding` tests it.
- Land next to a point of interest to interact: settlements (survivors board one by one, hold the LZ; settlements and hideouts inside the fence or within 12 m of it walk to the base by themselves and move in),
  supply crates (fuel, ammo, scrap), weapons caches (a weapon or one item card), relay towers (reveal nearby POIs),
  crash sites (scrap and a permanent boost). Zombie dens spawn an abomination boss when approached.
- The run starts without a base: the heli carries a founding crew of 4, 60 scrap and basic supplies. Pick a site with room for the 5x5 helipad (fields and town edges are fine, ideally near water), land and press **B** to found the FOB there: helipad, field workshop, the crew moves in (2 on Build), and the first assault comes two nights later. Until then there is nowhere to refuel or drop survivors.
- Landing on the base pad refuels, rearms, repairs, delivers passengers and opens the base menu (turrets, depot, armory, workshop, buildings, perimeter).
- The base has needs: everyone eats 1 food and drinks 1 water a day; with no beds left (2 in the hangar, 4 per Barracks) people camp outside, eat double, work at half rate and get hurt in assaults. Buildings (Barracks, Field Clinic, Generator, Well, Watchtower) and perimeter stages are placed as blueprints in the BUILD tab inside the fence and built by Build workers. The injured need a clinic bed and 1 medicine (pharmacies, crates, crash sites) or may die; water comes from wells, the Water job (river or lake nearby), water towers in towns, crates and lake huts. A fuelled Generator lights the base at night, keeps turrets at full rate, speeds healing and thins out the night hordes. Militia only shoot with rifles (weapons caches, crash sites, the Armory, zombie drops). The first run starts a short tutorial (also on the title screen; ESC menu skips it).
- Base life and menu: every rescued survivor (up to 60 drawn) steps off the pad, changes into uniform in the barracks (the hangar until one is built) and then goes about their job: militia patrol the sandbag line with rifles and stand on the defence posts during assaults, fuel/ammo/repair/workshop/build crews shuttle between hangar, depot and blueprints with a tool and pistol, farmers walk the fields, water carriers go to the river, medics stay by the clinic, the injured lie in it; at night everyone but the militia sleeps inside (campers by the fence). The base menu shows a status column on the left (population, stocks with per-day change, power, next assault) and BUILD / WORKERS / HANGAR tabs on the right; disabled buttons say why on hover (`scripts/base_pawn.gd`, `scripts/base_ui.gd`).
- People and morale (`scripts/people.gd`, `scripts/morale.gd`): every survivor is a named person (names from `data/names.txt`, fixed by the run seed) with a skill (Mechanic, Farmer, Medic, Soldier, Builder, Driver, rare Engineer) and maybe a trait (Tough, Glutton, Lazy, Brave, Insomniac). The WORKERS tab still sets how many work each job; the best-suited people take it (matching skill works x1.5, x1.75 at Lv 2 after 5 days, x2 at Lv 3 after 15; Soldiers on militia posts hit harder) and lists everyone (sort by job, name, skill or level; filter by job, skill, idle, injured or pinned; each row shows skill, trait, job, work rate); a click shows the person. A person can be pinned to a job in their row: they keep it (the job's count rises to cover them, and cannot go below its pinned people), the automatic assignment only fills the rest, an injured pinned person is off duty until healed, and Unassign all clears the pins. Base morale (0-100, start 60) is settled each day from hunger, thirst, campers, deaths, power, beds, assaults and big rescues, and each day it also drifts a quarter of the way back to 60 (so it settles where the base's conditions put it); under 40 work slows, under 25 people may leave at night, over 75 work speeds up. The status column shows morale and its three main reasons; a full cabin shows who is aboard on the HUD.
- Salvage: crates, crash sites, scrap yards (industrial towns, some suburbs) and kills yield metal, parts and electronics; with the Workshop upgrade, Workshop workers break 12 units a day each down into scrap, the cheapest first (metal 1, then parts 2, then electronics 3), keeping 10 parts back for heli repairs.
- Building: pick a tool in the BUILD tab and the menu gives way to a build bar along the bottom (**1-9** switch tools, **LMB** place, **RMB** remove / cancel, **R** rotates a lone sandbag, **ESC** back to the menu). A ghost on the 16 px grid shows what goes where, green if it fits, red if not, with its cost. Everything goes on open ground inside the fence (fields, yards and roads too) and is built by survivors on Build duty.
- Sandbags (5 scrap) join their neighbours into straight runs, corners and T pieces; zombies have to chew through them, Militia man them during assaults, Medics patch the hull for free. Barbed wire (3 scrap) can be walked through, but zombies in it crawl and bleed until it wears out. **Demolish** marks a house, hedge or garden fence inside the fence: Build workers tear it down for scrap (a house also gives metal and parts).
- Outposts and allied villages (`scripts/outposts.gd`): on the base pad **O** loads 2 militia into the cabin (they are passengers, landing at the base does not unload them; **O** again puts them back). Land at a gas station, checkpoint or radio mast and press **O**: 40 scrap and the crew found an outpost (at most 1 + built watchtowers). Its pad refuels and rearms the heli from the outpost's stock and saves the run; gas stations add fuel and checkpoints ammo every day, and **O** on the pad deposits spare fuel and ammo or, with a crew aboard, reinforces the garrison (up to 6). Garrisons defend against night raids (zombies) and bandit raids; an outpost that is overrun falls and part of its garrison dies. **P** beside a cleared settlement that still has people makes it an ally: it leaves food and water every 2 days, collected by landing there and delivered on the base pad, and falls if unvisited for 8+ days or raided. Outposts and villages show on the minimap (green held, orange hurt or neglected, red blinking under attack). Tuning constants are at the top of the file.
- Trade with allied villages (`scripts/trade.gd`, window `scripts/trade_ui.gd`, numbers in the consts at the top): land beside an allied village and press **T** (touch: the TRADE button) to open the trade window (the game pauses). Each village has a small offer per day, the same for a given seed, village and day: a recruit with a skill (70-140 scrap, on 60 % of the days), electronics x5 (45), medicine x2 (35), rifles (50), food x10 (15). Every purchase raises that good's price (+25 %, recruit +40 %, food +10 % of the base price, at most x2.5) and the heat falls 10 % per day. Surplus food and water sell at poor rates (10 for 4 / 3 scrap, -20 % per sale, never below 40 %, the base keeps 3 days of supplies). Offers and prices save per village (hook `trade`); `SMOKE=trade` tests it.
- Convoys (`scripts/convoys.gd`, constants at the top): build a **Motor Pool** (80 scrap, BUILD tab, hotkey 8 in the build bar; Demolish is now 9), then trucks from salvage (20 metal + 15 parts, 2 per Motor Pool) in the **CONVOY** tab of the base menu. Pick a destination (a town or landmark the heli has seen within 900 m, an allied village or an outpost), a driver (Driver skill; higher levels drive faster) and up to 3 escorts (militia or soldiers, one rifle each). The truck drives the road network there, loads for 45 s and drives back: food, water, salvage, fuel or ammo by site kind (less on each later trip to the same site), a village's stock or an outpost's surplus. The crew is away from the base (no work, no food) but counts for the score. Zombies on the road slow the truck and chew its hull; bandits wait in ambush at bridges and narrows, more often near a camp: the truck stops, the radio calls "Convoy under attack" with a bearing, fly there and clear them (the escorts shoot back). A destroyed truck loses its cargo and rifles, its crew dies or walks home injured. Convoys show on the HUD and the minimap (yellow, red blinking under attack).
- Infected (`scripts/spawn_table.gd` holds every enemy type and its spawn weights by danger, day, region and town; `scripts/infected.gd` the abilities): **screamers** (day 3+) stop, wind up for 3 s and scream, which calls every zombie within ~600 px (shoot them first); **spitters** (day 4+) keep 80-120 px away and spit acid at sandbags, wire and a landed heli; **crawlers** (day 2+, forests and lakes) crawl through barbed wire unharmed and are a smaller target (use flak or splash); **bloaters** (day 5+, towns) burst into gas and hurt people in the base if they die at the fence; from day 10 **armored brutes** stop 70 % of shots into their front (flank them with Q/E) and horde spawns are sometimes runner packs. Hits knock zombies back, flash white and splatter; corpses stay for a minute (max 120); headshots do double damage (Optics raises the chance); heavy guns shake the camera (switch off in the ESC menu).
- Assaults (`scripts/assault.gd`, consts at the top): they come in 2-3 pushes with a pause to reload and patch the line, and grow faster after day 25. Attackers still out there at dawn the next morning give up and scatter. From the 2nd assault a theme (radio and HUD show its name): runner storm, brute ram (brutes walk straight at the base and smash sandbags and houses), screamer chain (more zombies keep coming while a screamer lives), spitter siege, mixed. The evening before, a Watchtower radios the main direction ("movement north-east") and the HUD draws an arrow at the fence; 70 % of the attackers come from there (with or without a tower). Every 9th night from founding is a **blood moon**: red sky, a boss for sure, a bigger wave, 2x XP, the night does not end until the last attacker is dead (at most 8 minutes of held clock), morale +10 once it is held. `Assault.night_taken(day)` says whether an assault falls on a day (bandit raids keep out of it).
- Bandits (`scripts/bandits.gd` camps and raids, `scripts/bandit.gd` riflemen, MG nests, RPG gunners, `scripts/technical.gd` pickups; unit counts in `scripts/spawn_table.gd`): from day 4 bandits hold prisons, checkpoints, motels and quarries, more of them the farther out and the later. Unlike zombies they shoot the heli in the air: keep moving and keep your distance (hits fall off with range, speed and darkness). Riflemen run for cover behind houses, hedges and woods, where half your rounds miss; an RPG gunner aims for 1.5 s (red line, radio warning), then fires a homing rocket: break hard or drop a flare (every flare level pulls rockets off). Technicals drive the roads with an MG. Bandits and zombies fight each other, and the gunfire draws more zombies. Camps send patrols that carry people off from settlements; intercepted bandit radio announces raids on settlements (dusk) and, from day 12, on the base (at night, never on a zombie assault night). Camps also raid outposts and allied villages within reach at dusk (announced the same way). Base raiders give up and leave about 75 s after they set out. Kill a camp's whole garrison to clear it for good: XP, +8 morale, freed prisoners to pick up and a stash (rifles, fuel, ammo, scrap) when you land there.
- Radio missions (`scripts/missions.gd`, list `scripts/missions_ui.gd`): from day 2, with a base, Command radios a mission every ~2.5 min (at most 3 open, **J** shows the list with direction, time left and reward; the HUD compass and minimap mark them; accepting means flying there, the timer running out drops it; targets lie 0.9-4 km from the base, and once every site in reach has had a mission they come round again). **Rooftop rescue** (a family on a roof in a town: winch them or land in the yard), **Black box** (land at an airliner crash site; salvage and a key part), **Silence the screamer** (a screamer and guards near a town that calls a horde each night), **Escort** (refugees walk to the base while you stay close). Rewards: scrap, XP, morale, often a person with a rare skill (Engineer, Driver). **Clear the camp** (a bandit camp within reach: done when it is cleared) and **Medicine run** (bring 2 medicine to an allied village; a village you have neglected for 6 days asks by itself, the run fails if the village falls). Missions save with the run.
- More base buildings (`scripts/buildings2.gd`, constants at the top; BUILD tab and the build bar, mouse / touch only, hotkeys 1-9 are unchanged; the BUILD tab list scrolls): **Field Hospital** (110 scrap, add-on to a built Field Clinic, one per clinic: +4 beds, patients heal 1.5x faster), **Greenhouse** (70: +5 food a day without farmland, drinks 4 water a day), **Forge** (100: a Forge job for 2 workers, Mechanics and Engineers are good at it; 3 scrap make a metal, 2 metal a part, up to 260 of each (enough for any wreck repair), never spends the last 30 scrap), **Bunker** (130, one: when the base is breached the survivors hide there once, the wave breaks off and the hull comes back at 30 % with 2 hurt, the bunker is spent; not against the final siege; assault losses weigh half on morale, a held assault gives +4), **Radio Relay** (100, one: 1.5x map reveal, radio hints and mission radius, bandit raids on the base are heard a day early). Save with the run; older saves load.
- Late-game buildings, the scrap sink (S1; `scripts/buildings3.gd`, constants at the top; build bar and BUILD tab, mouse / touch only, unlocked from day 20 or perimeter level 2, hotkeys 1-9 unchanged): **Fortress Wall** (400 scrap, one: sandbags become walls with double hit points, the pieces standing and every new one, tinted), **Flak Emplacement** (500, two at most: every 0.8 s for 3 base ammo it shoots down an RPG or gunship rocket within 340 px, else hits the gunship for 50, else bursts on a crowd of 6+ for 16 splash; **12 scrap upkeep a day**), **Command Post** (600, one: missions pay +25 % scrap and XP, a fourth mission may be open, and the Warlord's gunship / the Alpha's assault are radioed shortly before they come; **8 scrap upkeep a day**), **Supply Vault** (300, one: the fuel and ammo stores hold 50 % more, a base breach hurts half as many and costs half the morale). Upkeep is paid at dawn; short of scrap the building idles until the next dawn that finds it paid (radio line). Test: `SMOKE=late`.
- Evacuation, the optional victory (`scripts/evac.gd`, constants at the top, `scripts/victory_ui.gd`): from day 15 Command picks up a military frequency. Build a **Radio Station** (BUILD tab: 90 scrap, 6 electronics, an Engineer at the base to run it) for contact, then an **Airstrip** (150 scrap): a straight 4x24 cell strip inside the fence (perimeter level 1+, off the pad and hangar, **R** turns it; roads, fields and yards are fine, trees on it are felled, houses must be demolished; the ghost says what is in the way). Finishing it starts a 3-day countdown, then the **final siege**: the biggest wave of the run in 4 pushes with 2 bosses, plus a bandit raid, at dusk. The transport lands after midnight and needs 60 s to load, which only runs while no zombie or bandit is on the strip (a second push and raid come with the landing); hold it. Then choose **END RUN** (the run ends as a victory) or **STAY BEHIND** (the run goes on endlessly, the victory stays counted on the title, the profile and the leaderboard, a second evacuation is not possible). Whoever never builds the airstrip simply plays on. The airstrip is base-only (not at outposts). Saves with the run.
- Pace is deliberate: landing and take-off spool for 1.5 s, gunfire and the rotor make NOISE that draws zombies (flying is safe, landing is not: everything within 380 px swarms a landed heli), settlements fall overnight if you leave them, the base eats food (farm workers, crates) and hull repairs cost scrap.
- Fuel and ammo run out. Dying ends the run; survivors rescued across runs unlock permanent seats, fuel and ammo capacity (saved per profile in `user://profiles/<id>/meta.json`; volumes stay global in `user://settings.json`).
- Ironman save (`scripts/save.gd`): the run autosaves (landing on the base pad, each new day, every 120 s, closing the window or hiding the browser tab; also in the middle of an assault, never during the tutorial) into `user://profiles/<id>/run.sav`. The title offers **CONTINUE**; **NEW RUN** over a saved run abandons it (counts as lost). Death deletes the save. New systems hook in with `Save.register(key, save_fn, load_fn)`; the world is replayed from the seed plus a chunk/founding event log (kept for POI ids; generation itself does not depend on load order) and a tile edit log (`chunks.edit`).
- Heli wear (`scripts/wear.gd`, constants at the top): rotor, engine, gearbox and door gun (condition 0-100 %) wear in flight and from hits (bandit hits three times as much). Under 50 % they bite (engine: slower, rotor: the gun spreads, gearbox: more fuel, gun: jams); an engine under 25 % may quit: 3 s of alarm, then an autorotation glide of 8-12 s with weak steering, **SPACE** puts her down (open ground costs some hull, forest, water or houses a lot; passengers may get hurt) and the crew repairs on site (45 s, faster with a mechanic or engineer aboard) before take-off with a 30 % engine. At the base the HANGAR tab shows the four bars and orders repairs: the base crew works while the heli sits on the pad and spends salvage parts (10 % per part).
- Bosses (Q12, `scripts/bosses.gd`, constants at the top, spawn days and odds in `SpawnTable.BOSS_ROWS`, codex entries under Enemies): every one is telegraphed with a red ring, lane or circle plus a sound, shows an HP bar, and changes pattern at half HP. The **Matriarch** (roams from day 6) rears up for 1.6 s and spawns a brood; hurt, she flees for 12 s healing and returns with a bigger brood. The **Juggernaut** (day 10+) is plated all round (80 % less damage) except the red fuel pack on its back; it turns slowly, so fly round it. When it lowers its head, leave the red lane: it rams houses, sandbags, the base and a landed heli, and a crash leaves it dazed; at half HP the plates come off and it charges twice. The **Hive** (day 8+, in many power plants and stadiums) throbs, then calls a wave every 26 s while you are within 1000 px, spitters, bloaters and armoured brutes too at half HP; destroy it for good. The **Warlord** (day 10+, holds some far prisons, quarries and harbours) has bodyguards and a mortar that shells a LANDED heli: a red circle shows the spot, lift off before it lands. Rewards: 2-3 boss kills each (item unlocks), morale, scrap, parts or electronics (the Hive and the Warlord), rifles. `SMOKE=bosses` tests them.
- Heli wrecks, types and run modifiers (`scripts/wrecks.gd`, `scripts/heli_types.gd`, `scripts/modifiers.gd`, HANGAR rows `scripts/fleet_ui.gd`, constants at the top): airfields, checkpoints and airliner crash sites may hold a downed heli (Huey, Little Bird, Black Hawk, Chinook; the Hind only at checkpoints). Hover slowly over it and press **G**: after 4 s of steady hovering it is on the hook. With a load the heli flies at half speed (Chinook -15 %), burns double fuel, cannot land, the load swings behind, snags in trees and gets chewed by zombies and shot by bandits; **G** lets go (inside the fence it goes to the HANGAR, elsewhere it stays for later). The HANGAR tab starts the repair: salvage (e.g. 200 metal / 120 parts / 60 electronics), 1-3 key parts (rotor head, turbine, avionics from missions, which bring the one the wreck in the fence still lacks, and military landmarks) and 10-20 work days of Mechanics on Repair duty (Engineers x2). A repaired heli is parked at the base and switched in at the pad; items, weapons, loadout, wear and hull stay with each heli. Each type you repair once can be picked as the start heli under NEW RUN on the title, next to the run modifiers (each a score factor, multiplied, at most x3, sent to the leaderboard): Dense Horde (500 kills), Warlords (reach day 8), Scarcity (3 runs), Dry Season (reach day 12), Glass Cockpit (5 assaults held), Blackout (3 boss kills); the unlock is in brackets.
- Arsenal (Q8; `scripts/weapons.gd`, `scripts/progression.gd`, `scripts/burn.gd`, `scripts/systems/thermal.gd` and `smoke.gd`, numbers in the tables and consts at the top): six new hardpoint guns, each a hangar unlock, level 1-5 like the others: Scatter Gun (10 wide pellets, damage fades with range), Flamethrower (11 m stream, zombies burn for 3 s), Grenade Launcher (lobbed shell that bursts at the mouse, flies over zombies), Precision Rifle (pierces 2, favours headshots), Sonic Cannon (cone that stuns and shoves, no effect on bandits) and Net Launcher (roots zombies in the burst, bosses shrug it off); plus the systems Thermal Sight (night heat view) and Smoke Launcher (pops when you are shot at, rockets lose their lock) and the modules Extra Tank and Heavy Plates (armour for weight). Burn, stun, falloff and lob are bullet properties, so `enemy.gd` has no new branch. `SMOKE=arsenal` tests them; `tools/gen_sprites.py` draws the icons.
- Progression (`docs/PROGRESSION.md`, `scripts/progression.gd`): the heli has hardpoint / system / module slots (start 1/2/2, Workshop or meta unlocks raise them to 2/3/3).
  Each level-up offers three item cards (new item or next level, max 5): **1-4** or click picks, **R** rerolls, **S** skips for 15 scrap, **B** banishes (unlocked at 400 rescued).
  Heli level caps at 30, further XP turns into scrap. The base HANGAR tab scraps items; the title screen HANGAR (**H**) lists every item and its unlock condition.

## Dev
- Version: `config/version` in `project.godot`, history in `CHANGELOG.md`. `bash tools/deploy_web.sh` exports the Web build to GitHub Pages, stamps it with commit and date and tags the commit `v<version>` (bump the version for a new release).
- `python tools/gen_sprites.py` regenerates the art ("Field Map" style) in `assets/sprites/b/`. Previews: `docs/region_*.png`.
- `python tools/gen_sfx.py` regenerates all sound effects in `assets/sfx/`. Sound starts muted for now: **M** or the ESC menu turns it on.
- Profiles and leaderboard on the title (`scripts/profiles.gd`, `profile_ui.gd`, `leaderboard_ui.gd`): the first start opens **ENLIST** (callsign checked live by the name filter, portrait from a 4x3 grid), **PROFILES** (or the portrait top left) switches, creates, renames and deletes profiles, **LEADERBOARD** (**L**) shows DAILY / SEASON / ALL-TIME (POINTS / DAYS / RESCUED / KILLS / BEST RUN) with your own row highlighted and a click-through breakdown. Everything local works offline; a profile created or renamed offline is registered / renamed when `Online.online_changed(true)` fires, and the title shows `Online.title_notice()`.
- Player-name filter: `scripts/name_filter.gd` (`NameFilter.check(name)` returns "" or the reason). Word lists in `data/` (banned words partly from LDNOOBW, CC BY 4.0, see `data/ATTRIBUTION.md`); `python tools/gen_wordlist_sql.py` writes the matching server inserts to `server/supabase/zz_wordlists.sql`.
- Headless checks (bash): `SMOKE=1 godot --headless --path . --quit-after 30000` prints `SMOKE OK`. Other values: `poi`, `base`, `ui`, `prog` (heli systems), `tut` (tutorial), `nav` (pathfinding), `found` (founding the base; the other tests start with a base founded at the start site), `town` (world gen: a city, towns joined by road, road bends), `filter` (player-name filter), `save` (save, reload, load: same world and state; - Save robustness (`scripts/cloud_save.gd`, `scripts/save_data_ui.gd`, server `009_cloud_save.sql`; constants at the top of `cloud_save.gd`): an **online profile** uploads its saved run (`var_to_bytes` + zstd, ~2-3 KB early, cap 2 MB) to the cloud when you land at the base, at most every 5 minutes (`Save.autosave(true)`; slot = `Save.slot`, default `"run"`, file `<slot>.sav`). The title offers **RESTORE FROM CLOUD** when there is no local run but a cloud one (needs the profile's online login, which lives in `auth.json`: cleared browser data makes the cloud copy unreachable). Death or NEW RUN deletes the cloud copy first, then the local one; a delete that fails offline is kept in `cloud_del.json` and retried (until then nothing is uploaded or restored). **BACKUP** on the title exports the active profile (meta + run) as a `.dgsave` file (the online login only if ticked, it works like a password) and imports one as a **new profile**; an imported run is **never ranked** (its online state is dropped, the title says "Imported - not ranked"). Web: download/upload through `JavaScriptBridge`, desktop: the native file dialog. Test: `SMOKE=cloudsave`.
- Daily seed and weekly season (Q14; `scripts/online.gd` `fetch_daily` / `start_daily`, title DAILY panel in `scripts/title_ui.gd`, server `010_daily_season.sql`, test `SMOKE=daily` + `server/test_010.sql`): the **DAILY** panel on the title shows today's start heli and modifiers (the same for everyone, chosen by the server from the UTC date and a secret), the countdown to the reset at 00:00 UTC and whether you played today. **PLAY DAILY** asks the server for the seed (only at the start, so nobody scouts the world beforehand) and reloads into the run: **the first start of the day is ranked**, later ones are practice (not sent); a claimed start that does not reach the server within an hour counts as practice. Same world and modifiers, not the same run (spawns and loot stay random). Online profiles only, never offline. The daily saves into its own slot (`daily.sav`, cloud slot `daily`) so the normal run stays; the title shows **CONTINUE** for both and **RESTORE FROM CLOUD** for either slot. **LEADERBOARD** has DAILY (today), SEASON (points of all runs of the ISO week, UTC) and ALL-TIME (the old tabs). Same-world check across platforms: `SMOKE=daily` pins the tile hash around the start for seed 20261102 (`WORLD_HASH` in `tests/daily_test.gd`); the web build prints it once as `WORLDHASH seed 20261102: <n>` in the browser console when opened with `?perf=1`, and the two numbers must match.
- Music and sound (`scripts/sfx.gd`, `tools/gen_music.py`, `tools/gen_sfx.py`, consts at the top of `sfx.gd`): the music is a state machine over seven 16 s stems of one tempo and key (120 BPM, A minor), so layers crossfade on bar lines: title, calm, tension (enemies near, night, evacuation countdown, engine alarm), combat (3+ enemies close), assault, blood moon and victory. A calmer state follows only after 8 s. Radio messages duck the music and are announced by a beep per sender (Base, Pilot, Watchtower, bandit radio, convoy; others the classic one); **ESC** has a **Radio beeps** switch. More effects: RPG warning, autorotation alarm, building knocks and chime, button clicks, event gong (`Sfx.play("event")`). All sounds are generated by the two Python scripts (no third-party audio); `SMOKE=music` tests it.
- Online (`scripts/online.gd`, autoload `Online`, backend in `server/README.md`): anonymous Supabase sign-in (tokens in `user://profiles/<id>/auth.json`, refreshed, one retry on 401), `register` / `rename` / `set_avatar` / `delete_me`, `fetch_board` / `fetch_player`. A run is sent when it starts, at each new day and when it ends or is abandoned; if the first call at run start fails the run is **unranked** (saved with the run, never sent), a ranked run that loses the connection queues updates in `outbox.json`. Headless tests (`SMOKE`) never go online; `ONLINE_URL` overrides the server.
- Error reporting (`scripts/error_report.gd`, autoload `ErrorReport`, backend `server/supabase/008_errors.sql`, tests `SMOKE=errors` + `server/test_008.sql`): script errors, `push_error`/engine errors (a `Logger`) and in the browser `window.onerror` go to the `report_error` RPC, once per message and session, scrubbed of local paths, queued in `user://error_reports.json` while offline. Opt-out: ESC menu **Send crash reports** (default on, notice on the first start). Off under `SMOKE` and in editor builds. Release exports only have GDScript backtraces with `debug/settings/gdscript/always_track_call_stacks`. Viewing the reports: `server/README.md`.
- World gen (`scripts/chunk_manager.gd`): nine regions (farmland, forest, lakeland, suburbs, wasteland, highland, river valley, coast, winter highland) with clearings, ponds, orchards, burned patches and copses inside. A macro grid of 5x5 chunks (~2560 px) holds one site each: a town (seven templates per region, rare cities with a 4-5 block street grid) or a landmark (gas station, military checkpoint, campground, quarry, airfield, radio mast, dam, crashed airliner, prison, motel, lumber camp, church ruin, highway with wrecks; Q10: hospital, harbour, stadium, power plant, ski lift ruin), or nothing; a town is always within ~1200 px of the start. Each site links to its two nearest neighbours by road (A* over terrain, paved between cities, fords over brooks, bridges over rivers). Gas stations, checkpoints and airfields have a fuel depot (fills the tank once). Generation is order-independent (`SMOKE=world`); other systems find sites with `chunks.sites_near(pos, radius, kind)`. Previews: `docs/region_*.png`, `docs/landmarks.png`, `docs/landmarks2.png`.
- Coast and winter (Q10): the coast has a sea (dark water: no landing, no walking) behind beaches, cliffs and dunes, fishing villages with boats at their jetties and harbours; the winter highland lies under snow with frosted woods, huts and frozen lakes you can land on. Landmarks (`scripts/landmarks.gd`): a hospital (pharmacies, its survivors join as medics, many infected), a harbour (fuel depot, boats, sometimes a bandit camp or an outpost), a stadium (a big horde pours out the first time you come close, crates and a weapons cache on the pitch), a power plant (landing at its stores takes a power part: base generators burn half the fuel; Q12 boss site, `sites_near(pos, r, "power_plant")`). Convoys loot all four, missions send you to hospitals and stadiums. **Save format 3:** the same seed now gives another world, runs saved before Q10 are discarded.
- Radio events (Q13; `scripts/events.gd`, all 34 cards in `scripts/event_cards.gd`, numbers in the consts at the top of `events.gd`): from day 2, every 5-8 minutes of play a card fits the state of the run (day, morale, stocks, buildings, bandit camps, at the base or in the air) and the radio asks what to do: 2-3 options, **1-3** or a click answers, the game does not pause, after 60 s the default option is taken (a locked default falls back to the first option you can afford). No random cards in a daily run. Options cost or give stocks, people (through the person facade), morale and bandit attitude, offer radio missions, bring zombies or a bandit raid, or start a chain (a follow-up card days later). Bandits who like you (attitude 2+) plan no random base raid. The card shares the `choice_ui.gd` queue with the level-up cards (never two at once, a level-up waits behind an open event and the other way round). ESC menu: **Radio events** switch. Other code: `Events.offer(id, ctx)`, `Events.register(id, card)`, signal `chosen(id, option)`.
- Rivals (Q15; `scripts/nemesis.gd`, numbers in the consts at the top): up to two named rivals per run, built on the Q12 bosses. A **rival Warlord** (from day 10) takes a known bandit camp, an **Alpha** (from day 8) is a named Matriarch that hunts the heli; names and traits (brutal, cunning, proud / swift, fecund, scarred) come from the seed, so a daily has the same rivals for everyone (only the ransom card is left out there, like all event cards). They **remember** how you play and adapt: flying fast most of the time brings RPG men and more gunship rockets, a lot of barbed wire turns the Alpha's assault push into crawlers and spitters, the gunship hovers at the edge of your most-used gun's reach, cleared camps make the Warlord's raids harder, breaking off a fight brings them back sooner. Below 30 % HP (cunning: 45 %) a rival **escapes** (twice at most) and comes back 1-3 days later 25 % stronger. **Escalation**: contact and a radio intro, two days later raids every two days on a convoy on the road, an outpost or village or a settlement, with the Warlord's ransom card (pay 80 scrap for 3 quiet days, or refuse), after two raids the big attack every four days: the Warlord's captured **gunship** (heli physics, 100 px/s, holds 140-380 px, 5-round bursts and rockets after a red aim line, holds its rockets while flares burn near you and breaks away from a flare near it) with a bandit raid on the base, on a night no assault has taken (he waits while the heli is parked and gives up at dawn: no escape counted, he keeps his damage); the Alpha leads the next assault. Killed for good: 150 scrap, 10 parts, 10 electronics, 400 XP, morale +15, a codex entry; the next rival of that kind 8 days later. `SMOKE=nemesis` tests it.
- Walkers (zombies, survivors, base pawns) path around houses, hedges, water, rock, sandbags and buildings with `main.gd` `nav_move` / `nav_path` (cached AStarGrid2D on the 16 px grid, straight line when free); walled-in zombies chew through after 2 s.
- **UI scale and HUD zones** (`scripts/ui_theme.gd`, constants at the top): "UI scale (90-150 %)" in the ESC menu. Every UI hangs under a `UiTheme.attach()` root that covers the canvas at that scale; colours, font sizes, spacing and touch minimums live there. HUD zones: top left status (bars, slots, info block with a [-] fold button), top right minimap, top centre alert line, bottom left radio log, bottom row key hints; the info block and the log only get the lines that fit around each other and other layers' panels (base menu, touch pads), the alert line hides when no room is left. The base menu folds its status panel when the main panel would not fit beside it, every tab scrolls; touch controls grow to 125 % at most, the title and dialogs only as far as they fit (title max 100 %, the HANGAR list scrolls). `SMOKE=uitheme` compares control rects at 90/100/150 %; `UI_SCALE=150 SMOKE=shot` takes the screenshots at that scale.
- Performance (`scripts/perf.gd`): **F3** toggles an overlay (bottom right) with FPS, frame time p50/p95/max over 10 s, "work" (frame time without the idle wait), enemy/bandit/POI/chunk counts and ms per frame in chunk generation, pathfinding and the spawner. In the web build `?perf=1` in the URL (desktop: env `PERF=1`) prints the same numbers every 10 s to the console and pushes them to `window.doorgunnerPerf`. `SMOKE=perf` runs the scenarios (city approach, blood moon with 250 attackers, bandit camp + horde, convoy SEND on a far route, long flight / memory) and checks the desktop limits (work p95 < 33 ms, chunk loading < 50 ms per frame).
- Long-run balance check (`tests/longrun_test.gd`, not part of the quick loop): `SMOKE=longrun godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 --quit-after 90000000` lets a bot play 40 days (flies to towns, rescues, delivers, staffs jobs, builds, holds assaults, founds and reinforces outposts, protects villages and picks up their food, sends convoys, hooks, tows and repairs wrecks and switches helis, flies rooftop / black box / medicine missions) and writes one row per day to `build/longrun.csv` (stocks, people, deaths, morale, kills, base hull, assaults, bandit raids, points, outposts, villages, convoys, wrecks, missions, evacuation, size of `run.sav`). `RUN_SEED=<n>` fixes the world; `LONGRUN_EVAC=1` pushes for the evacuation from day 15 and stays behind after the victory, `LONGRUN_MODS=dense,warlords` plays with run modifiers; `LONGRUN_DAYS`, `LONGRUN_OUT` and `LONGRUN_DEBUG=1` (bot log) are optional. `python tools/longrun_report.py build/*.csv` prints per-phase summaries (days 1-10, 11-30, 31-40) and outliers. It takes about 15-40 minutes; exit code 1 if the run collapsed.
- `SMOKE=shot godot --path .` saves screenshots (flight, combat, level-up cards, base, hangar, settings, key bindings, F1 help, title hangar) into the project folder. `SHOT_AT=suburb:city` (region, optionally `:template` or `:landmark`) starts them next to such a site, `SHOT_AT=airfield` next to the nearest landmark of that kind.
